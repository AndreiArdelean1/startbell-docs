# StartBell Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

StartBell rings a real alarm when your calendar meetings start, or a set time before. It works entirely on your device.

## What StartBell does with your data

- **Calendars.** With your permission, StartBell reads the calendars already on your device through Apple's Calendar framework (EventKit). It uses them to decide which meetings should ring. Meeting titles, times, calendar names and links are read and used on your device only.
- **Your settings, rules and picks.** They are stored on your device only. They are not synced to your other devices.
- **Alarms and notifications.** They are scheduled locally with the system (alarms through AlarmKit, with notifications as a fallback). Nothing is sent anywhere to schedule them.
- **Purchases.** StartBell has one optional in-app purchase, StartBell Unlimited. Apple handles the payment and the purchase record. StartBell stores only a yes/no flag on your device to remember that you unlocked it. The developer receives no payment or personal details.

## What StartBell does not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No server or backend operated by the developer.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
- StartBell does not send your data over the internet, to the developer or to anyone else.

## Diagnostics export

Settings has an Export diagnostics button. It creates a text file only when you tap it (settings, permissions, counts, times and the activity log, with no meeting titles, calendar names or locations). It goes wherever you choose to share it. The developer receives nothing unless you send it.

## Meeting links

If you tap Join on a meeting, the link opens in the video-call app or browser you use (for example Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or Webex). That service's own privacy policy applies from then on.

## Permissions

You can change or withdraw calendar, alarm and notification access at any time in iOS Settings. Without calendar access StartBell cannot see your meetings.

## Children

StartBell collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
