# StartBell Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

StartBell rings a real alarm when your calendar meetings start, or a set time before. It does its work on your device.

This privacy policy applies to the StartBell app for iPhone and iPad, together with its Lock Screen countdown and alarm controls, operated by Andrei Ardelean. Andrei Ardelean is referred to in this policy as the "Service Provider".

This policy covers the StartBell app only. The website hosting this page is run by its hosting provider, whose own privacy policy applies to visits.

## What StartBell does with your data

- **Calendars.** With your permission, StartBell reads the calendars already on your device through Apple's Calendar framework (EventKit). It uses them to decide which meetings should ring. It reads each event's title, time, calendar, organizer, your response, busy/free status and recurrence, and looks in the event's link, location and notes for a video-call link. All of this is read and used on your device. StartBell never changes your calendars.
- **What StartBell keeps on your device.** Your settings, calendar choices, rules and picks, and the information it needs to keep alarms in step with your calendar: meeting titles and times, calendar names, the meeting organizer (which can be an email address), event identifiers, a record of recent alarms, and a feed of changes such as moved or renamed meetings. Alarm records are removed 14 days after they ring. This information is stored in StartBell's private storage on your device and is not sent to the Service Provider or anyone else. Deleting StartBell deletes it.
- **Backups.** Like other app data, this information is included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have them turned on. These backups are handled by Apple or your computer, not by the Service Provider. StartBell does not sync it between your devices.
- **Alarms and notifications.** They are scheduled locally with the system (alarms through AlarmKit, with notifications as a fallback). Nothing is sent to the Service Provider to schedule them. Alarms, the countdown and notifications show the meeting title on your Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and on a paired Apple Watch, as iOS shows them.
- **Purchases.** StartBell has one optional in-app purchase, StartBell Unlimited. Apple handles the payment and keeps the purchase record. StartBell keeps on your device whether Unlimited is active and how many free alarms you have used. Apple does not give the Service Provider your name, payment details or any other way to identify you.

## What StartBell does not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No server or backend operated by the Service Provider.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs in the app.
- StartBell itself makes no network connections and sends none of your data to the Service Provider or anyone else. The only online activity comes from Apple's systems: the App Store, when you view, buy or restore StartBell Unlimited, and links you choose to open.

## Crash reports and statistics from Apple

If you have turned on sharing with app developers in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports and anonymous usage statistics about StartBell with the Service Provider. Apple controls this. You can turn it off at any time.

## Diagnostics export

In Settings → Activity log you can tap Export diagnostics. It creates a text file only when you tap it. The file contains the app version, your device model, iOS version, language/region and time zone, your StartBell settings and permission states, alarm times and counts, and the activity log. It contains no meeting titles, calendar names, locations or organizers. You choose where to send it. The Service Provider receives it only if you send it.

## Meeting links

If you tap Join on a meeting, the link opens in the video-call app or browser you use (for example Zoom, Google Meet, Teams or Webex). That service's own privacy policy applies from then on.

## Permissions

You can change or withdraw calendar, alarm and notification access at any time in iOS Settings. Without calendar access StartBell cannot see your meetings.

## Your choices and rights

Everything StartBell keeps is on your device (and in your backups, if you have them). Delete the app to delete it all, or remove a single pick or rule in the app. The Service Provider holds no data about you, so there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on its side apart from support emails (see Support and contact). The Service Provider does not sell or share personal information.

## Children

StartBell is not directed at children. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

## Changes

If the Service Provider makes a material change, it will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect, and mention it in the app's release notes.
